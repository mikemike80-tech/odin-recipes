Tinolang Manok

Description

Tinola is also one of the favorite during rainy season.

Ingredients

  • Whole Chicken
  • Garli
  • Onion
  • Malunggay Leaves
  • Hot Pepper Leaves
  • Bay Leaves

Steps

  1. Heat a cooking pot and pour-in cooking oil.
  2. Saute garlic and onion.
  3. Add ginger and chicken, and then cook for a minute.
  4. Pour-in water. let boil. Cook for 5 minutes.
  5. Put-in the malunggay leaves and hot pepper leaves. Cook for 3 minutes.
  6. Add fish sauce and stir
