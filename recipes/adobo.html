Adobo

Description

This is Adobo, one of the most popular dish in the Philippines.

Ingredients

  • Pork belly
  • Pepper
  • Vinegar
  • Soy Sauce
  • Garlic
  • Bay Leaves

Steps

  1. Put the cooking pot in the stove.
  2. Put the oil when the pan is hot.
  3. Put the garlic.
  4. Add the peppercorns and bay leaves.
  5. Put the pork belly in the cooking pot.
  6. Pour the soy sauce and vinegar
